    • Personalized TD Wealth Plan2

    • In-house and third-party due diligence of money managers, Mutual Funds and Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)

    • We align your investment objectives with your TD Wealth Plan

    • Ongoing advice based on your goals and objectives

Our disciplined process

We implement a disciplined approach to portfolio construction leveraging TD Wealth's Global Asset Allocation Committee3

  • Asset allocation strategy

    The Committee has three objectives:

    • Articulate broad market themes
    • Identify major risks on the horizon
    • Provide macro-level asset allocation direction

  • Manager selection

    • Quantitative Assessment: Quarterly and annually against benchmarks and peers
    • Qualitative Assessment: Ongoing discussions with investment managers
    • Identification of Material Changes
    • Material Change Process

  • Portfolio construction

    • TD Wealth is dedicated to working towards superior, risk-adjusted investment returns over the long term
    • TD Wealth manages downside risk through diversified security selection and fully integrated risk management
    • Portfolios are diversified across asset classes, geography, style and managers

Our process focuses on you

The depth of resources you expect. The personalized attention you deserve.

  • Tailored investment solutions

    Customized investment solutions that leverage our best thinking to manage volatility and risk, with an eye on helping you work towards long-term success

  • Monitoring and reporting

    We continually evaluate your portfolio and screen your fund managers, communicating with you frequently so you understand your progress

  • Professional money management

    Private Client Group clients can access the expertise of internal and external asset managers typically reserved for institutional clients

  • Investment manager vetting

    When outside expertise is needed for your investment plan, our process evaluates managers based on their strategies and track record
