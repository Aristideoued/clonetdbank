Private Banking

Comprehensive personal banking and lending solutions taken to the next level

Enjoy the highest level of local personal service and a streamlined banking experience all in one place with TD Wealth Private Client Group. As a Private Banking Client you have a dedicated Relationship Manager responsible for the day-to-day management of all your personal banking, lending and investing with access to the full array of TD offerings. Our services are best suited for those with $750,000 or more to invest with us which is a significantly lower minimum than our national competitors.

Whatever your financial needs, we'll work with you to create customized banking and lending solutions:

  • Liquidity

  • Deposit Solutions

  • Payment Solutions

  • Services for Business Owners

Liquidity management

Working with your Relationship Manager, you have access to a suite of premier lending choices for amounts of $250,000+ to fund your specific needs.

Enjoy flexible funding options to cover a variety of short- and long-term needs:

  • Liquidity for funding or leveraging investments
  • Real estate transactions
  • Bridging cash flow gaps
  • Asset acquisition
  • Personal and business investment
  • Refinancing high interest rate debt

Make the most of our lending resources that feature:

  • Minimum facility of $250,000
  • Interest only or amortizing payment terms available
  • Variable or fixed interest rate options
  • Timely approval process
  • Annual review required

TD Custom Lending Solutions

Get tailored borrowing solutions with pricing and collateral options based on your needs

  • Use eligible securities in your TD investment account as collateral for a loan or line of credit
  • Gain accessible liquidity while still working towards your long-term investment strategy
  • Streamlined application and approval process, with limited financial documentation for qualifying transactions

Learn more about TD Custom Lending Solutions

Deposit solutions

Enjoy concierge service and the security of knowing you're getting the most from your deposit accounts. Your dedicated, local Relationship Manager handles the daily oversight of your accounts based on your input and goals.

Checking and savings accounts that work for you
Benefit from tiered interest rates, which increase based on your balances, with our Private Tiered Checking and Private Tiered Savings accounts.
