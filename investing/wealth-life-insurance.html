Life Insurance

Single premium life insurance can help you plan your legacy and ensure a bright future for the special people in your life.

Protect your family's financial future

Single premium life insurance can be an important part of your financial plan with features including:

  • Tax-efficient transfer of assets to your beneficiaries
  • Lifetime protection
  • Coverage amounts of $15,000 to $1 million1
  • Common purchase age of 50-85
  • No medical exam required2
