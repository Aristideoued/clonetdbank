Private Client Group FAQs

TD Wealth Private Client Group requires that eligible clients have $750,000 or more in investible assets or $3 million or more in total net worth (exclusive of primary residence).

A dedicated TD Wealth Relationship Manager will serve as your primary point of contact and coordinate a team of TD Wealth Private Client Group professionals to assist you across various services, including:

  • Investment Management: Collaborate with an Investment Advisor to provide guidance on investment strategies and actively manage your portfolio
  • Financial Planning: Work with a TD Wealth Investment Advisor to develop a TD Wealth Plan that aligns with your goals and adapts to changing circumstances
  • Lending Solutions: Partner with a TD Wealth Lending Advisor to explore flexible options that meet your financing and cash-flow needs
  • Private Client Banking Services: Support services for TD Wealth Private Client deposit accounts, cash management, and other banking services to support your financial activities
  • Wealth Transfer and Estate Planning: Engaging with TD Wealth specialists to develop strategies for effective estate planning and wealth transfer

Clients of TD Wealth Private Client Group have access to a team that includes:

  • Relationship Manager: Serves as your primary TD Wealth contact who coordinates with the Wealth team to provide personalized, goal-based solutions
  • Investment Advisor: Offers advice on investment opportunities, portfolio management, and market trends to help optimize clients' financial portfolios
  • Wealth Lending Advisor: Experts who assist with customized lending solutions
  • Wealth Strategist: Assists your Relationship Manager in developing advanced planning strategies tailored to your individual goals
  • Client Service Associate: Supports your day-to-day needs, ensuring responsive service and smooth communication between your wealth management team
