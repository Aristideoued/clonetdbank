TD Commercial Banking Security Center

Enhance or develop your company's fraud prevention plan with these helpful tips.

How protected is your business?

The risk of fraud is always present.
While you can't predict when or how your business may be targeted, there are many steps you can take to reduce the chances of a fraud attempt becoming a fraud loss.1

Don't wait until your business suffers a loss to take action.
Now is the best time to better protect your business. Learn more about fraud prevention practices that you can implement to help protect your company's financial assets.

Download tips to help get you started

What to do if you suspect online fraud

  • Contact us immediately with details of the incident.

    Treasury Management Services Support
    1-866-475-7262
    Mon-Fri, 7:30AM – 8:00PM ET
    Sat, 9:00AM – 1:00PM ET

    TD Bank Fraud Resolution Group
    1-800-893-8554

  • Follow your company's escalation process for suspected PC or network compromises.

    • This may include shutting down your web browser or computer and disconnecting your Internet connection or wireless capability
    • Continued activity on a compromised computer could affect your entire network and expose additional sensitive information to criminals
