Franchise Finance Insights

According to TD Bank's 2023 survey1 of restaurant franchise operators and finance industry professionals across the U.S., we know investing is top of mind in 2024 with focuses on:

  • Embracing digital platforms, mobile apps, and online ordering systems to enhance customer experience (51%)
  • Leveraging technology to automate and streamline operations (51%)
  • Store reimagining or remodel to focus on quick-service restaurants (49%)
  • Reducing labor (30%)
