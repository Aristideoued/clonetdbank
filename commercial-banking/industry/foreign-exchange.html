How it works

Purchase or sale based on current value of foreign currency for settlement within two business days.

Purchase or sale of foreign currency for settlement on a future value date beyond two business days.

For a preset premium, obtain the right but not the obligation to purchase or sell a currency on a future value date at a set strike.

Purchase or sale of foreign currency for settlement in whole or in part over a customized range of future value dates.

Simultaneous purchase (sale) and sale (purchase) of foreign currency for two different value dates.

Benefits

Buy or sell currency as you need it.

Protect yourself from future fluctuations in foreign currency rates by locking in a rate for delivery on a future settlement date.

Mitigate your risks from future fluctuations in foreign currency rates while retaining participation in market moves.

Protect yourself from future fluctuations in foreign currency rates by locking in a single rate with flexibility to specify delivery over several settlement dates.

Enables you to reduce risk and limit trading costs by executing offsetting trades simultaneously.

Welcome to the TDFX trading platform.

Our online foreign exchange trading system provides real-time quotes and trading capabilities for over 25 currencies.

Our secure system preserves the confidentiality of your transactions, ensuring the integrity of the exchange information you submit online. TD Bank Group enforces trusted Internet and firewall protocols when you trade online. These protocols are continuously monitored and independently verified.

Each customer is assigned a unique user ID and must create a password that is secure and confidential.

If you trade on behalf of multiple companies, you can execute trades through each company using a single login profile.

To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" to answer a series of security questions.

The TDFX landing page lets you see our products and services, as well as helpful support links, at a glance

You can find today's trading hours displayed above the navigation bar throughout the platform.

The Request for Quote feature makes executing foreign exchange transactions online simple and convenient.

When you select your company, the product drop-down menu will customize with the products available to you. These may include spot transactions and forward contracts, window forward contracts, and foreign currency swap contracts.

Fill in the necessary information, including the desired currencies, the amount, and the trade settlement options available to you.

You can add an optional trade or wire memo to your transaction. A trade memo is only viewable on the TDFX platform. A wire memo will appear on the outgoing wire transfer.

When you're ready, click Request Quote. You'll be prompted to review and accept the trade.

The "Today's Trades" and "Window Forwards" blotter tabs display all completed trades and outstanding window forwards. Click the PDF icon to view and print a summary of your trade.

On the TDFX trading platform, you'll have access to:

  • Transaction history,
  • up-to-date TD economic reports,
  • rates and foreign exchange research,
  • expert market commentary and
  • helpful product tips to help facilitate your transactions
