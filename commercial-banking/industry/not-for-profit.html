TD Not-For-Profit Financing

Financing and treasury management solutions customized for needs of not-for-profit organizations.

The TD Not-for-Profit Financing advantage

  • In-depth industry expertise

    You'll work with highly trained and experienced bankers who specialize in not-for-profits. They'll develop strategies, offer proactive advice and create a plan tailored to your needs.

  • Integrated, customized solutions

    With the resources of the whole bank at your service, you can access a full suite of products designed to fit your day-to-day needs, match long-term goals and help your organization grow.

  • The strength of a top bank

    With one of the highest credit ratings of North American banks, TD's strong financials allow us to stick with your organization through economic and business ups and downs.

Whom we serve

We provide specialized solutions for not-for-profits (designated with a 501(c)(3) tax filing status) with annual sales revenue of $5 million and above. Segments served include:

  1. Healthcare

  2. Educational

  3. Religious

  4. Human services

  1. Cultural institutions

  2. Advocacy and industry groups

  3. Affordable and transitional housing

Lending Solutions

Whether you're financing construction or expanding into new markets, your TD Not-for-Profit Specialist can help you choose from our comprehensive selection of lending options:

  • Working capital lines
  • Construction financing
  • Lease financing
  • Term loans/bridge loans
  • Commercial mortgages

Our public finance capabilities can also help satisfy your short- and long-term taxable and tax-exempt capital needs, reduce borrowing costs and flexibly respond to the changing economic environment.

Liquidity Solutions

Designed for not-for-profits with annual sales revenue above $5 million

  1. Conveniently manage all operating and strategic funds in an "all-in-one" solution

  2. Eliminate the need to transfer between accounts manually

  3. Access advanced treasury management services

  4. FDIC deposit insurance of up to $250,000 per depositor

TD Not-For-Profit Advantage Checking

Offset cash management fees and earn interest on all excess balances.

  1. Easily initiate, track and receive electronic payments

  2. Eliminate the need to manually transfer funds between accounts

  3. Earn a competitive interest rate on excess balances

  4. No transaction limitations

  5. Accrue earnings credit rate (ECR) and offset the cost of services
