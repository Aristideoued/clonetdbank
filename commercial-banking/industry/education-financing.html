TD Education Financing

Customized financing solutions for educational entities including nonprofit, public and private colleges and universities, schools for grades K–12 and specialized schools.

The TD Education Financing advantage

  • In-depth industry expertise

    You'll work with highly trained and experienced bankers who specialize in education. They'll develop strategies, offer proactive advice and create a plan tailored to your needs.

  • Integrated, customized solutions

    With the resources of the whole bank at your service, you can access a full suite of products designed to fit your day-to-day needs, match long-term goals and help your educational institution prosper.

  • The strength of a top bank

    With one of the highest credit ratings of North American banks, TD's strong capital position allows us to maintain deep client relationships through even the most turbulent business cycles.

Education segments we support

TD Education serves educational entities including:

  • Nonprofit public and private colleges and universities throughout the country
  • Schools for Grades K–12 located within the TD footprint from Maine to Florida
  • Specialty schools located within the TD footprint from Maine to Florida
 

Customized solutions to fit your needs

Credit

  • Fixed or variable rate direct bank purchases
  • Lines of credit
  • Term loans
  • Bridge financing


Liquidity

  • Direct pay letters of credit
  • Standby bond purchase agreements
  • Hybrid liquidity facilities

TD Securities

Working with our TD Securities partners, educational organizations also have access to full-service investment banking capabilities, including:

  1. Taxable and tax-exempt bond underwriting

  2. Variable rate debt and commercial paper remarketing services

  3. Direct structured debt solutions

  1. Primary and secondary market trading

  2. Guaranteed investment contract bidding

  3. Derivatives
