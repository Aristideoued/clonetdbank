Share the green when you refer a friend

You'll both get $50 with qualifying activities.1 Refer up to 10 friends a year and earn $500.

Refer a Friend to TD Bank

If you're a fan of TD Bank, now you can share the love when you refer a friend, family member, coworker or neighbor. TD Checking accounts are designed to make everyday banking better with easy-to-use digital tools and a new TD Bank app. Plus we have longer hours at our locations and thousands of ATMs so you can bank on your schedule. And, when you refer a friend, you'll both get $50!1

How to refer a friend

  • 1

    You can request a personalized code at any TD Bank. Simply ask one of our associates to generate your personal referral code.

  • 2

    Share your personalized code with your referral however you like to connect.

  • 3

    Have your friend visit a nearby TD Bank and present your code when they open their TD Checking account.

How to cash in 

After your friend opens their TD Checking account at a TD Bank, they'll need to have at least $250 in qualifying direct deposits or make 15 qualifying Visa® debit-card purchases within 60 days of opening the account. Then you'll both get $50 credited into your account no later than 95 days from the new account opening date.

What is a qualifying direct deposit?
Qualifying direct deposits are limited to electronic direct deposits of your payroll, pension, or government benefit payments (such as Social Security) of at least $250 each.

What is a qualifying debit-card purchase?
Qualifying purchases are those made using a TD Visa Debit Card to buy goods and services. ATM withdrawals made do not qualify. Returns and other adjustments may affect qualifying purchases.

By participating in this program, both the referring customer and newly referred customer may be aware that the other is a TD Bank customer.

The primary owners of the new personal checking account and the referring customer's personal checking account may be required to complete a valid IRS Form W-9 or substitute IRS Form W-9 to provide certain identifying information. You may be subject to backup withholding at the rate specified in the U.S. Internal Revenue Code if you fail to provide that information or meet certain other conditions. TD Bank may issue the primary owners of the new personal checking account and the referring customer's personal checking account an IRS Form 1099 or other appropriate forms reporting the value of the bonus. Offer may be withdrawn at any time and is subject to change. 

Additional Requirements for Referring Customer:

The referring customer must have an existing TD Bank personal checking account and will be provided with a unique Share the Green offer code that they may choose to share with friends. Referring customer can receive a $50 bonus for each friend they refer that opens an account using their unique Share the Green code, and who qualifies as outlined within. Referring customers may receive up to ten (10) Share the Green Bonuses ($500 total value) within a calendar year.

Additional Requirements for Referred Customer:
Newly referred customers do not include customers that have an existing TD Bank personal checking account OR had a previous personal checking account that was closed within the preceding 12 months OR have received a prior personal checking account bonus at any time.

To qualify for the $50 Share the Green Bonus, the newly referred customer must provide their friend's unique Share the Green offer code upon account opening. The newly referred customer must: 1) open a TD Bank personal checking account at a TD Bank Store; and 2) receive a qualifying direct deposit of at least $250 into the new checking account within 60 days of account opening OR complete a minimum of fifteen (15) qualifying TD Bank Visa® Debit Card purchases within 60 days of account opening. Qualifying direct deposits are limited to electronic direct deposits of your payroll, pension, or government benefit payments (such as Social Security) of at least $250 each. Person-to-Person and bank transfers between your TD Bank and any other accounts you have at other financial institutions or brokerages are not considered a direct deposit. Qualifying Visa® Debit Card purchases are those made using a TD Visa® Debit Card to buy goods and services. Withdrawals made at the ATM do not qualify. Returns and other adjustments may affect qualifying purchases. Qualifying purchases are determined in the sole discretion of TD Bank and must post to the new account within 60 calendar days of account opening.
