Learn about your TD Choice CD renewal

When your TD Choice CD matures, it will automatically renew to the same length term at the non-promotional interest rate in effect at the time of renewal. Starting on the date of maturity, you'll have a 10-calendar-day grace period to make changes.

See today's TD Choice non-promotional rates

How to make changes:
To renew your CD, call 1-800-643-5363 or visit a TD Bank. To move your funds into a TD account, add or withdraw funds, visit a TD Bank. Learn more about your renewal options or download our CD renewal FAQs (PDF).

Non-Promotional TD Choice CD rates

Your CD will automatically renew on the day your CD reaches maturity. Starting on your maturity date, you’ll have a 10-calendar-day grace period to make any changes, including withdrawing funds without penalty, making additional deposits, changing your CD length term, or renewing your CD.

Your CD will automatically renew* to the same length term at the non-promotional interest rate in effect at the time of renewal. The new Interest Rate and Annual Percentage Yield (APY) for your CD will be based on the rates available at the time of your CD's renewal date. If you do not have a TD Choice Promotional CD, you will benefit from contacting us to renew into a TD Choice Promotional CD.

Connect with us by finding a TD Bank store near you or by calling 1-800-643-5363 to talk about your options and choose the account that's right for you.
