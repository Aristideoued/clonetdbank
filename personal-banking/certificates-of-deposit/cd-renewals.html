How TD Choice CD Renewal Works

As your TD Choice CD approaches the end of its term, you may be wondering what happens when it matures. We're here to help you plan your next step.

What happens when my CD matures?

  1. We'll let you know when it's time to renew
    You'll start to receive renewal notifications up to 2 months prior to your CD's maturity date. Log in to Online Banking or the TD Bank app to see your rate and term under your CD account details

  2. You'll have 10 days to make changes
    Starting on the date of maturity, there is a 10-calendar-day grace period where you can make changes, including renewing your CD, without being assessed a fee.1 Explore our promotional CD rates and terms

  3. At maturity, your CD will auto-renew2
    Your TD Choice CD will automatically renew to the same length term at the non-promotional interest rate in effect on your maturity date if you don't take action during the grace period

What are my choices if I want to make changes?

We can work with you to the renew your CD – check out our promotional rates and terms to find the right fit for your savings goals.

  • Explore our promotional rates and terms

    Call or visit a TD Bank to renew your CD and take advantage of the TD Choice Promotional CD rates and terms available.

    See rates and terms

  • Move your funds to another TD Bank account

    Earn a competitive rate and access your money anytime with a TD Savings account. Visit us in person to learn more.

    Explore accounts

  • Add or withdraw funds

    Visit a TD Bank to add or withdraw funds from your CD. You can also make a partial withdrawal when you visit your local Store.

Non-Promotional TD Choice CD rates

Your CD will automatically renew on the day your CD reaches maturity. Starting on your maturity date, you’ll have a 10-calendar-day grace period to make any changes, including withdrawing funds without penalty, making additional deposits, changing your CD length term, or renewing your CD.

Your CD will automatically renew* to the same length term at the non-promotional interest rate in effect at the time of renewal. The new Interest Rate and Annual Percentage Yield (APY) for your CD will be based on the rates available at the time of your CD's renewal date. If you do not have a TD Choice Promotional CD, you will benefit from contacting us to renew into a TD Choice Promotional CD.

Connect with us by finding a TD Bank store near you or by calling 1-800-643-5363 to talk about your options and choose the account that's right for you.
