We're committed to getting things right.

If you think there is an error, would like to send a request for information, or have a credit bureau dispute, please write to us as soon as possible.

In your letter, be sure to include:

  • Your name, property address, and account number or other identifying information
  • Description of error or information you are requesting
  • Why you believe there is an error
  • Any additional information you think that will help us

Send your letter to: TD Bank, Credit Disputes, PO Box 71466, Philadelphia, PA, 19176-1466

A delay may be experienced if the letter is not mailed to the specified address.
