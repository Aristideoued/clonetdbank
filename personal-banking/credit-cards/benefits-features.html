Credit Cards

Explore Your TD Bank Visa® Credit Card Benefits

Every TD Bank Credit Card comes with an added layer of protection such as emergency card replacement, roadside assistance and more.

  • Signature Card Benefits

  • Business Card Benefits

  • Platinum Card Benefits

Visa Signature Card Benefits1

In addition to your TD Bank Credit Card rewards, as a Signature cardholder you'll get all the Visa benefits of a Platinum card, plus a range of security and travel benefits:

  • Fraud and protection benefits
  • Travel benefits
  • Entertainment and lifestyle benefits

Fraud and purchase protection benefits

  • Cell phone protection2
    Get reimbursement for your stolen or damaged cell phone when you pay for your wireless phone bill with your TD Bank Visa Credit Card
  • Emergency card replacement
    If your card is lost or stolen, you can get an emergency replacement card sent immediately
  • Purchase Security3
    If an item you purchased using your new credit card is stolen or damaged due to fire, vandalism or accidentally discharged water or certain weather conditions within the first 90 days, Purchase Security can replace, repair or reimburse the eligible item
  • Extended Warranty Protection3
    Extended Warranty Protection will add up to one additional year of coverage on select items that you purchase with your TD Bank Visa Credit Card
  • Your partner against identity theft4
    Your TD Bank Visa Credit Card includes ID Navigator Powered by Norton LifeLock, providing tools so you can act quickly if your identity is threatened

Travel benefits

  1. Visa concierge
    Your 24/7 Visa concierge can assist you with travel planning, entertainment, sending a gift to someone special and more

  2. Roadside Dispatch®
    Access 24/7 roadside service with this pay-per-use program. Certain terms and conditions apply

  3. Travel and emergency assistance services5
    A single phone call gets you the help that you need anywhere in the world

  4. Lost luggage reimbursement3
    When you pay for your common carrier ticket with your TD Bank Visa Credit card you can be reimbursed up to $3,000 for your checked or carry-on luggage if your luggage is lost due to theft or misdirection by the Common Carrier

  1. Visa luxury hotel collection
    Enjoy special guest status at Visa Signature Luxury Hotel properties all around the world

  2. Signature experience benefits6
    Discover premium benefits at golf courses around the world and exclusive access to select Sonoma County wineries

  3. Car rental benefits7
    Enjoy special savings, complimentary rental upgrades (when available), waived fees and more from Avis and Budget, and with Audi on demand you can experience up to 15% off (with a guaranteed minimum discount of 10%) on Audi rentals at all Audi on demand locations when you use your TD Bank Visa Signature card

Entertainment and lifestyle benefits

  1. Explore your creativity on Skillshare8
    Discover new skills, deepen existing passions, and get lost in creativity. Enroll your TD Bank Visa Credit Card to get unlimited access to thousands of classes with 3 months free and 20% off a Skillshare annual membership

  2. Discover new music with Sofar Sounds9
    Use your TD Bank Visa Credit Card to get access to 7-day Visa Exclusive Presale opportunities to select Sofar Sounds shows plus a free ticket per show with purchase of one or more tickets during the presale window. Offer Terms apply

  3. Same-day delivery from a variety of stores you love10
    Enroll your TD Bank Visa Credit Card to get 3 months of free Shipt membership, then 9 months of membership at 50% off, with free delivery on orders $35+

Learn more about your TD Visa credit card benefits in the Visa Guide to Benefits (PDF)

Business Solutions Credit Card Benefits1

In addition to your TD Business Solutions Cash Back Rewards, as a Signature cardholder you'll get a range of security, travel and spend management benefits:

  • Fraud and protection benefits
  • Travel and entertainment benefits
  • Visa Spend Clarity for Business

Fraud and purchase protection benefits

  • Cell phone protection2
    Get reimbursement for your stolen or damaged cell phone when you pay for your wireless phone bill with your TD Bank Visa Credit Card
  • Purchase Security3
    When you use your TD Business Solutions Credit Card to purchase an eligible item, Purchase Security can replace, repair or reimburse your item up to $10,000 per claim and $50,000 per cardholder within the first 90 days in the event your item is stolen or damaged
  • Extended Warranty Protection3
    Extended Warranty Protection will add up to one additional year of coverage on select items that you purchase with your Business Solutions Credit Card1
  • Your partner against identity theft4
    Your TD Bank Visa Credit Card includes ID Navigator Powered by Norton LifeLock, providing tools so you can act quickly if your identity is threatened

Travel benefits with your business card

  • Roadside Dispatch®
    Access 24/7 roadside service with this pay-per-use program. Certain terms and conditions apply
  • Auto rental collision damage waiver3
    When you use your TD Bank Visa to rent a vehicle, you’ll get built-in coverage for physical damage, vandalism and theft, reasonable and customary towing2
  • Travel and emergency assistance services5
    A single phone call gets you the help that you need anywhere in the world

Visa Spend Clarity for Business

Keep business spending on track

  • Empower your business with easy-to-integrate, intuitive tools –
    Quick and easy set-up that integrates with existing systems and requires no external support
  • Control spending, whenever and wherever it happens –
