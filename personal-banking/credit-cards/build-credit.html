Building credit FAQs

Yes, a secured credit card may help you build or rebuild your credit. A secured credit card is designed for individuals who haven't started building their credit history – like a recent high school or college graduate – or those who want to improve their credit. By using the card responsibly, you could help build a good credit history and increase your credit score.

A secured credit card allows you to charge expenses like any non-secured credit card. The difference is that a secured card is backed with a cash deposit you make with the bank or card issuer that serves as collateral for your secured credit card. Typically, the collateral amount will be your credit limit as well.

At TD Bank, if you are conditionally approved for the TD Cash Secured Credit Card, you'll open a TD Simple Savings account and make a deposit to set your credit limit, up to your conditionally approved amount.

As you use your card and make payments, TD Bank reports your credit card activity to the three major credit bureaus. If you use the account responsibly and keep it in good standing, you can build a positive credit history and may be eligible to graduate to an unsecured card. Please keep in mind, not all accounts qualify for graduation. 

The credit limit for a TD Cash Secured Card is $300–$5,000, subject to credit approval. If you are conditionally approved for the card, you'll open and fund a TD Simple Savings account as your security deposit.

You can deposit a minimum of $300 up to your conditionally approved amount to set your credit limit.

A secured card lets you charge expenses and requires monthly payments like an unsecured card, but it's secured by the cash in your linked savings account. You are required to keep your purchases within your savings deposit amount. With an unsecured card, the card issuer assumes, based on your credit history and other factors, that you will be able to make at least the minimum monthly payment every month. Your creditworthiness helps the card issuer to determine the credit limit.

To apply for the TD Cash Secured Credit card, you'll need to be at least 18 years old and a U.S. resident.

You'll also need to provide the following info:

  • Full legal name
  • Date of birth
  • Current address and whether you rent or own and if you've recently moved
  • Social Security Number
  • Gross annual income
  • Employment information
  • Bank account information

Learn more and apply online for the TD Cash Secured Card or visit a TD Bank near you to apply in person.
