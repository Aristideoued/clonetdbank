Business Solutions credit card FAQs

You apply for a TD Business Solutions Credit Card as you would any other credit card, but the application may require more information, both personal and business. And it's important to remember, a business card can only be used for commercial or business purposes, not personal or household expenses.

To apply for a TD Business Solutions Card, you'll need the following information, and you may be asked to provide additional information as needed:

  • Personal information: Name, address, Social Security Number (SSN), phone number, email
  • Personal annual income
  • Business contact info: Phone number, email, mailing address
  • Legal name and type of business: LLC, S- or C-corp. For a sole proprietor, use the business owner's legal name
  • Business federal tax ID: If you've formally registered your business, this is your nine-digit federal employer identification number (EIN). If not, use your SSN
  • North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) industry code: The application may provide a list of NAICS codes to choose from, but research your code beforehand to have it ready
  • Business legal structure: Corporation, partnership, nonprofit, LLC, cooperative, etc. You'll include business owners/controllers as well. If you're an unregistered one-person business, it's probably a sole proprietorship
  • Business revenue: This should be your prior-year annual revenue before taxes/expenses
  • Years in business and your role: Your personal position in the business, like owner or partner

Here are some of the benefits a TD Business Solutions Credit Card:

  • Cash back on everyday business purchases, with no rotating categories or limits
  • Add employee cards at no cost, with the ability to set spending limits
  • Set up controls and reporting with Visa Spend Clarity
  • No annual fee*
  • Paperless billing and online BillPay
  • Fraud protection against unauthorized use
  • Emergency card replacement
  • Cell phone protection6

Generally, anyone who has a business can apply for a TD Business Solutions Card. This includes sole proprietors and non-profits.

Yes, while you don't need to have a business license to get a TD Business Solutions credit card, you do need to have business expenses. If you earn money under your Social Security Number through a part-time hobby or as an independent contractor and have business expenses, you can apply for a TD Business Solutions credit card. You will also need to meet the other criteria for getting the card, which varies depending on the bank or card issuer.

A small business credit card helps make your bookkeeping more efficient – plus, with the TD Business Solutions Credit Card, you can earn unlimited 2% Cash Back on your business purchases. A business card keeps your business and personal expenses separate, lets you track business expenses, and manage cash flow all in one place.
