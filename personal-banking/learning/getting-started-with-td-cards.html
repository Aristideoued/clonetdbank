TD Bank Visa® Debit Card

Your debit card helps you get the most out of your TD Bank accounts—here, there and everywhere. Make purchases and pay bills in person, online, over the phone or in the TD Bank app. Plus, visit thousands of TD Bank ATMs across the U.S. and Canada to access your money. And if you ever need a replacement, you can stop in a store for a new card.

Manage your TD Bank accounts online

View your debit card transactions and manage all aspects of your TD Bank accounts with Online Banking. Set up services like Bill Pay, send money to friends and family, transfers and personalized alerts.

Need a debit card? Send us a secure message from within Online Banking, and we'll mail you a new card.

Manage your debit card with card controls

All the tools you need to manage your debit card are available in Online Banking or the TD Bank app.

  1. Activate your new card
    Easily activate your card within Online Banking or through the TD Bank app

  2. Lock or unlock your debit card in seconds
    Misplaced your card? You can easily lock it, then unlock it when it turns up. Our quick, interactive tutorials can show you how it works

  3. Replace your card, instantly
    If your card is lost, stolen or damaged, order a replacement through the TD Bank app and have your card instantly issued to your digital wallet while you wait for your new card to arrive in the mail

  1. Change your PIN
    If you forget your PIN, you can change it in just a few simple steps

  2. Request a limit increase
    Request a temporary debit card limit increase to cover large purchases, and you’re good to go
