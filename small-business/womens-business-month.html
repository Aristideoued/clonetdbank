    Barefoot Books, Inc.

    Concord, MA – Nancy Traversy didn't see many culturally diverse children's books on the market in the early '90s. So she decided to make them herself, co-founding Barefoot Books. Her dream of pioneering children's publishing was moving along nicely, until the 2020 pandemic hit. Her business needed financing. Enter TD Relationship Manager Renee Durning, who provided the right solutions.
