Transcript

Paycor, a payroll and HR services provider preferred by TD Bank, is here to simplify your life. We've taken the feedback of more than 30,000 product designers, our clients, which has helped us build a solution tailored for you. We know your job is so much more than payroll, and you have a lot of people to keep track of: Hiring, Employee Administration, Time, Benefits, Pay Rates, Personal Info and more. Now you can do it all from one place. It is your cloud-based command center, providing simple stepped processes for everything from running payroll to hiring and onboarding a new employee. It makes what used to be a frustrating and complex process simple. So no matter where you login, you always have the most up-to-date version of what you need, in an easy to access, easy to use format. Your pay grid offers a comprehensive one-look view of your payroll where you can make edits, calculate pay totals and seek help. You can even make company or employee changes without starting the payroll over. So you can get things done quickly on your schedule, and without losing your work. In fact, you and further simplify employee administration by building an employee database. Paycor payroll and HR gives you the ability to add unlimited custom fields. Which means you can keep track of uniform sizes, certifications or anything else you can imagine. And best of all, you can run reports on any of it. From simple birthday or anniversary lists to an analysis of labor and compensation costs, all of which can be accessed by your CPA. Paycor, a payroll and HR services provider preferred by TD Bank is here to make life easier. It's a whole new way to look at payroll. It’s a whole lot more than payroll.
