Merchant Solutions1

Best for small and large businesses looking to process their customer payments in-store, curbside, over the phone or online. 

  • Keep cash flow moving with next-business day deposits5
  • Waive your monthly fees when you open or upgrade to a TD Business Premier Checking account
  • Accept all major credit or debit cards and contactless payments like Apply Pay, Google Pay or other mobile wallets

Merchant Solutions is a great option for restaurants, retail stores, healthcare offices, large corporations and professional services like salons. 

Learn more

TD Online Accounting2

Best for businesses who want to send online invoices and accept a wide range of digital payments with any major credit card, debit card or ACH.

  • Collect on your invoices faster
  • Payments go directly into your TD Small Business Checking account, typically within 1-2 business days.6
  • Access simple, automated accounting tools to easily track payments

TD Online Accounting is a great option for landscape and construction companies, property management companies, non-profits and consultants. 

Learn more

Zelle®3

Best for companies who want to send and receive money instantly without worrying about invoices.

  • Receive payments fast–typically within minutes.3
  • Share your email address, U.S. mobile number or QR code and customers can pay you wherever you are
  • Receive and send money right from Online Banking or the TD Bank app

Zelle® for small business is a great option for personal and professional service companies like hair stylists, trainers, pet sitters and more.

Tap to Pay on iPhone4

Best for companies who want to receive payments instantly and easily using just their iPhone. Tap to Pay on iPhone is available by enrolling in TD Online Accounting services.

  • Accept contactless card payments easily–anytime, anywhere using just your mobile device
  • Easily collect card, phone or wearable payments and receive payments instantly

Tap to Pay on iPhone is a great option for micro and small businesses, freelancers and consultants.

It’s no secret that the world of commerce is changing and increasingly shifting online. Today, most business-to-customer transactions are made using something other than cash. Because of this shift, it’s important to ensure you’re making things as easy as possible for your customers and not losing sales.

Zelle® is a Payment network where money gets transferred from one person's bank account to another person's bank account, in near real time. Users log into their banking portal and use a mobile phone number, email address, or QR code to identify the receiving party.
