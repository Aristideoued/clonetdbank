The Johnson Brothers Story shared by TD (US LOGO) Bank

It all started in our kitchen. Just us, giving the best fades in town.

Today, No Grease Barber Shop is a franchise, with 13 locations and a barber school.

The National Urban League introduced us to TD Bank, and their financial expertise helps us continue to grow and reinvigorate our local communities.

TD Bank is proud to support Black-led organizations and Black-owned businesses. It’s part of our commitment to a more inclusive and sustainable tomorrow.

Me and my brother had been cutting hair since we were 11, 12 years old.

We started No Grease! because it was a passion. My mom was a stylist, so it was a natural fit for us to kind of go into business.

The challenge is just bringing your dream into fruition.

The business started with me and my brother cutting hair across from each other, looking at each other, hoping and praying that we could keep the lights on. With maybe $10,000 coming in annually to 26 years later, you have 13 locations, a barber school, and you’re bringing in $9 million. So that's been the journey.

A mentor of mine, his name is Mr. Gordon from Philly, helped me get my start here in Charlotte. And so we're taking his blueprint and we're sharing it with our barbers and everyone around us focus on your community, building some type of mentorship program where you are paying it forward by giving it back to young entrepreneurs, young men and women.

I say mentorship is like the cheat sheet. It's the cheat sheet to success. If you got a good mentor, then they can help you navigate through the journey.

We've had the Barber school since 2003. I would say we've graduated well over two, 3000 students.

The feeling I get when I think about the lives and the careers that we've been a part of over the last 30 years is a sense of pride and humility.

No Grease! is not your ordinary barbershop. It's not what we do, it's how we do it.

From the time that people walk into our shops it’ what they feel it’s what they see it’s what they hear.

We make our clients feel like family. They feel at home. It's more than just a haircut.
