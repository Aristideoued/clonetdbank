Funding Opportunities

Our funding opportunities support programs that bring people together and give them access to important resources.

  • TD Charitable Foundation

    Our charitable arm that makes grants to non-profits to help sustain programming in the communities we serve

  • TD Ready Challenge

    A grant initiative focusing on innovative solutions aligned to the TD Ready Commitment

  • TD Bank Community Sponsorship Program

    Support for events and activities from organizations that build awareness of TD and positively impact our communities

How the 2024 TD Ready Challenge works

In 2024 we asked for applications from non-profit organizations with innovative approaches to supporting underserved small business owners who were developing, launching or growing a business.

We encouraged applicants to present new strategies, technologies or partnerships with the potential to disrupt systemic barriers for entrepreneurs, helping to create a more inclusive and supportive entrepreneurial landscape.

  • Hot Bread Kitchen

    Awarded $1,000,000

    The HBK Incubates program provides holistic and coordinated support services for low- to moderate-income women of color in New York City to seed, start and scale their food businesses.

  • PA CDFI Network

    Awarded $1,000,000

    PA CDFI Network's Drive Your Business program will help address gaps in services and reduce barriers faced by underserved entrepreneurs by combining technology, education, coaching and community collaboration.

  • Tampa Bay Chamber Foundation

    Awarded $1,000,000

    To help enable continued growth for small minority-owned businesses, Tampa Bay Chamber Foundation's Empower Sustainability Focused Accelerator program will use an innovative approach to strategic planning and execution, and leverage automation tools.

  • Washington Area Community Investment Fund (WACIF)

    Awarded $1,000,000

    By combining an innovative financial product with specialized support for entrepreneurs that's sensitive to difficult financial situations, WACIF's Resilient Futures program will be providing a first-of-its-kind initiative in the Washington, D.C., area.
