Capacity Building Fund

Nonprofit organizations are the backbone of our communities, providing essential services like job training, arts programs and housing assistance. We offer eligible organizations grants for training, talent, tools and tactics to further help their programs succeed.

How to apply

  • Before applying, take a look at the complete guidelines for our programs—including eligibility, timelines and frequently asked questions.

    Capacity Building Fund Guidelines (PDF)

  • You can apply for the Nonprofit Training Resource Fund at any time—we accept applications on a rolling basis and review them monthly.

    You may apply at any time Apply now*

  • You can apply for a Capacity Building Grant twice a year according to our funding calendar.

    Questions about the Non-Profit Training Resource Fund Apply now*

  • Questions about the Capacity Building Grants or Nonprofit Training Resource Fund? We can help.

    Questions about the Non-Profit Training Resource Fund Contact us
