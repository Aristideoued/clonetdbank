Celebrating Pride, and progress, all year long.

Celebrating who you are is part of who we are—and we've been doing it for years across North America.

A place for pride

Everyone deserves a place where they feel welcomed and accepted for who they are. That's why organizations that foster inclusive spaces for the LGBTQ2+ community are so important—and why we're so proud to support them.

Showing up for the community—with pride.

Forging a career through mentorship

Early in Jennie Platt's career, a mentor showed her she could bring her whole self to work and still move ahead. Now our Chief Marketing Officer, Jennie's focused on allyship and mentorship programs to make sure all our colleagues can feel seen, welcomed and valued—as their full selves.

Helping trans entrepreneurs thrive

Our colleague Barton Jackson created the NYC-based program Own It! to help trans entrepreneurs like Dani Fiore thrive. Read more to see why we're thrilled to sponsor this initiative that helps pave the way to success in the business world.

Empowering the community, in the Bronx and beyond

Destination Tomorrow supports the LGBTQ2+ community through financial education, health services, housing and more—and we're proud to have worked together for almost a decade. Founder Sean Coleman shares how our support is helping them expand their reach.

Creating a space to thrive

We put our all into supporting our colleagues' careers—and it shows.

I started volunteering at an LGBTQ plus safe space because I wanted to make meaningful change in my community.

I know what it's like to feel broken and isolated and different.

You see all of the wonderful things that happen here.

You also see all of the horrible things that can happen to our community, you know, Makes me so sad.

Having a place to find belonging is crucial.

I love watching people come into the space and go from being kind of very reserved and very shy to feeling really comfortable and really welcomed and really empowered and proud of who they are.

When I come into this space, I feel joy.

I feel safety.

I feel a younger version of myself being healed and that's more powerful than I can describe in words.

That's the power of community.
